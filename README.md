Three Tabs — my honest count of open browser tabs, for make.fun's ["How Many Tabs Do You Have Open?"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-danu-how-many-tabs-do-you-have-open-d86e783d) bounty.

The entry is one number: **3**. The field will skew big — every entrant chasing "most surprising" reaches for a hoarder number. Against that field, a very low count is the surprise: far lower than anyone guesses, still completely believable, and it hints at a life without a word of explanation. Three tabs is a working set: inbox, document, and this page. A person who keeps exactly three tabs is a person who closes things.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the single digit `3` to `/output/entry.txt` (stdlib only, no network, deterministic).
